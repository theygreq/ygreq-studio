1a Ember

Warmth earned, not poured

The field stays near-black; colour lives inside the letterforms — warm clay and olive light drifting behind the mark. Colour returns only at section breaks below. Filmic grain on every surface. Gambarino scales up as you descend. The most restrained of the three — closest to the documentary register.

Visual creative practice — Sofia, Bulgaria Scroll
1b Field

Colour from the first frame

The hero is a cobalt field; warm light moves through the mark instead of the other way round. The page then travels through full colour fields — cobalt, paper, clay, olive — as architecture, not punctuation. Boldest, most graphic. Biggest type contrast between the serif and the small tracked labels.

Visual creative practice — Sofia, Bulgaria Scroll
1c Spectrum

Colour, alive in the mark

Colour drifts slowly through the wordmark — cobalt to olive to clay — and recurs as living fields down the page. Type registers shift more often between Inter and Gambarino to keep the scroll engaging. The scroll cue and cursor are kinetic. The most dynamic, most colourful direction.
