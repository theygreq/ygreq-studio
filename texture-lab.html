1a Refined

Lighter, with a glint

Background is now a soft charcoal gradient instead of flat black. Grain is stronger and more present — tonal noise blended with overlay so it reads as film, not static. And a chrome-like glint sweeps across the wordmark itself, drifting slowly at rest and accelerating as you scroll — the first signal that something special is in store.

Visual creative practice — Sofia, Bulgaria Scroll
2a Fibre

Hahnemühle

Soft fibre mottling plus a fine matte tooth, the way the paper itself looks before anything is printed on it. Entirely static — nothing moves, nothing refreshes. Warmest and quietest of the three.

Detail, 8×

Being good isn't enough anymore.

2b Snowball

Grand Seiko dial

Fine granular flecks at varied brightness, the way a snowflake dial scatters light unevenly, plus a very slow raking highlight that drifts across the field over twenty-odd seconds. No fibre underneath — just texture and light. Most luxurious, but consider it can compete with photography if overused.

Detail, 8×

Being good isn't enough anymore.

2c Combined

Fibre, with light

Hahnemühle's fibre base carrying the snowball's slow raking light at lower intensity. Reads as paper at rest, and catches the eye faintly as you scroll past it. My pick of the three for the bulk of the site, with 2b reserved for something smaller and more deliberate — a single card or section, not the whole page.
