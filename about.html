YGREQ is a visual creative practice based in Sofia. It's built on a simple observation: most of the time, what you say and how you say it matters more than what you're actually doing. I work with a handful of brands and institutions each year on closing the gap between the two.

Origin

Seven years in London shaped the eye — studying photography at Westminster, shooting commercially for fashion and real estate, building a documentary practice exhibited and awarded internationally. The return to Sofia sharpened the argument, through communications work for the British Embassy and Khan Academy Bulgaria. YGREQ.STUDIO is what happens when all of it finally has its own name attached.

The practice runs alongside a full-time role at the British Embassy in Sofia. That dual setup is deliberate — it keeps the studio selective and means every engagement is one I've chosen to take on, not one I needed to.

How I work

The criterion is simple. I only take on brands I'm excited about — where I'd already be a customer, where I understand what they're reaching for, where the taste and instincts are already aligned. What I do from there depends on the brand. Some need their existing identity translated for a new audience. Others are ready to build something from the ground up. Either way, starting from alignment is what makes the rest work.

Photographer · Creative Director · Founder

Yassen is a Sofia-based photographer and creative director with a background in documentary photography and a working knowledge of the luxury and hospitality markets gathered across seven years in London. He founded YGREQ.STUDIO to bring photography, creative direction, and brand identity together under one practice — and to work selectively with the brands and institutions worth the attention.
